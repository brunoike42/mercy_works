Mercy Works Charity Organization

The Mercy Works department is a vital initiative of Hope Comprehensive, built on the principles of mercy, love, and social responsibility. We ensure that the healing ministry reaches those who need it most across Uganda.

Our mission is to extend the healing of Hope Comprehensive Hospital beyond its walls — bringing medical care, education, food relief, and shelter to vulnerable communities throughout Uganda.

We envision a Uganda where every person has access to dignity, health, education, and opportunity — where mercy is not just a word but a lived reality for all.
