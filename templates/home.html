About Us

Mercy Works Charity Organization

The Mercy Works department is a vital initiative of Hope Comprehensive, built on the principles of mercy, love, and social responsibility — ensuring that healing reaches the most vulnerable.

Our mission is to extend the healing of Hope Comprehensive Hospital's medical ministry beyond its walls, bringing health, education, and relief to communities across Uganda.
